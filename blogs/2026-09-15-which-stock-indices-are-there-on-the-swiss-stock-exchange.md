Swiss stock indices such as the SMI, SPI, or SLI each measure different segments of the Swiss equities market. Those watching the evening news almost always hear the same sentence: “The SMI gained X percent today.” But the SMI is only one of more than a dozen indices on the Swiss stock exchange. This overview shows what options are available and how they differ.
