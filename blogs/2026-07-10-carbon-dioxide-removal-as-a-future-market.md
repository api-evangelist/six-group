Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) is regarded as essential in order to achieve net zero. This has the potential to generate a market worth billions. Bjarne Steffen, professor at ETH Zurich, explains how certificates for durable CO₂ removal, CDR credits for short, will become a tradeable investment class.
